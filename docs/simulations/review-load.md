# Review-load simulation

> **This is a simulation, not a finding.** It shows how daily workload scales with the
> new-card cap under the assumptions below. It was not measured on a real learner.

Generated by `pnpm sim:review-load`. Do not edit by hand.

## Assumptions

- **Scheduler.** Core's `applyReview` (ts-fsrs, FSRS-6, library-default parameters, fuzz off). The script does not reimplement scheduling.
- **Target retention.** 0.9, constant for the whole run.
- **Recall model.** At each review, recall is a seeded Bernoulli draw with probability equal to the scheduler's retrievability at that moment. Recalled gives Good; not recalled gives Again. Hard and Easy are never used.
- **First review.** A new card's first review counts as recalled (Good).
- **Randomness.** Seeded mulberry32, base seed 20261121, offset by the new-card count so each setting has its own stream.
- **Seconds per review.** **8 s, a placeholder.** Replace it with measured data from your own sessions. Minutes scale linearly with it.
- **Horizon.** 365 days from 2027-01-01. Load is still creeping up slowly at the end of a year, so the late-window average approximates steady state and slightly understates the long-run load.
- **Session.** One session per day starting at 08:00 UTC. The learner reviews everything due, including cards that come due again within the session, then adds the day's new cards. The backlog guard never fires.
- **No missed days.** Real use has gaps, and gaps build backlog.
- **Card mix.** Every card is one kind. There is no production-card gating, so the three-cards-per-word effect is not modelled.
- **Uniform cost.** Every review takes the same time. Real time varies by card kind, hints and typing.
- **No content limit.** There is always another new card to introduce.

## Candidate default

The largest option whose average load stays at or under 30 minutes is **20 new cards/day** (25.9 min average, 29.2 min peak).
The plan's goal is 15 to 30 minutes a day. Staying at or under 30 on average leaves room for backlog after a missed day and for real reviews being slower than the placeholder.
